INSTANT Inc. — Company Handbook (Sample Document)

1. Company Overview
INSTANT Inc. was founded in 2019 and provides AI-powered analytics tools for
retail businesses. The company has 240 employees across three offices: San
Francisco (HQ), Austin, and Berlin. Annual revenue for 2023 was $18.4 million,
up from $11.2 million in 2022.

2. Product Lines
- InstantInsight: a dashboard product for retail sales analytics. Released 2020.
- InstantForecast: a demand forecasting tool using time-series ML models.
  Released 2022. Currently the fastest-growing product line, with 65% revenue
  growth year over year.
- InstantAgent: an AI customer support agent product, released in early 2024.

3. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Full-time remote work
requires manager approval and is limited to roles marked "remote-eligible" in
the HR system. All employees must be available during core hours, 10am-3pm in
their local office's timezone.

4. Vacation Policy
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per year, plus 10 company
holidays. Vacation carries over up to a maximum of 5 unused days into the next
calendar year. Employees must submit vacation requests at least 5 business
days in advance for requests longer than 3 days.

5. Performance Reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Reviews are
based on: (1) goal completion against quarterly OKRs, (2) peer feedback
(minimum 3 reviewers), and (3) manager assessment. Promotion cycles align
with the September review.

6. Engineering Practices
All engineering teams use a two-week sprint cycle. Code changes require at
least one peer review approval before merging to the main branch. The
company's core backend services are written in Python and Go; frontend
applications use React and TypeScript.
